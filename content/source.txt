 President Trump’s Second Term: Promises Made, Promises Kept, Results Delivered
President Donald J. Trump has executed the most sweeping restoration of American sovereignty, strength, and prosperity in modern history. With ferocious speed and resolve, President Trump has reversed decades of decline, kept more promises than any modern President, and delivered results for working families that once seemed impossible.
 
Here are 127 Promises Made, Promises Kept from President Trump’s second term:
Ending the Border Invasion and Restoring American Sovereignty
PROMISE MADE: “We will close the border. We will stop the invasion of illegals into our country.” (10/12/24)
✅ PROMISE KEPT: Under President Trump, illegal crossings at the southern border have collapsed to the lowest levels in American history after his rapid action to close the border on Day One. In FY 2026, Border Patrol recorded just 98,708 southwest border apprehensions — a 95% drop from the more than 1.8 million per year under Biden — and nationwide encounters fell to the lowest in the agency’s history.
PROMISE MADE: “I will send troops to the southern border to repel the disastrous invasion of our country.” (1/20/25)
✅ PROMISE KEPT: On Day One, President Trump declared a national emergency at the southern border and deployed thousands of military personnel to protect it — establishing National Defense Areas where illegal crossers can be charged with trespassing on a military installation.
PROMISE MADE: “I will immediately end the Biden border nightmare that traffickers are using to exploit vulnerable women and children.” (7/21/23)
✅ PROMISE KEPT: The number of unaccompanied illegal migrant children crossing into the country remains near a record-low, with Customs and Border Protection encounteringjust 763 unaccompanied minors at the southern border in August 2026 — down nearly 95% from the 14,237 encountered in August 2023 under Biden.
PROMISE MADE: “325,000 children are missing or dead. They came through the border… 325,000 children went through her stupid open border, and now they’re either missing, dead, or slaves. If you want to end this disaster, you must get out and vote.” (11/1/24)
✅ PROMISE KEPT: After the Biden Administration lost track of 325,000 migrant children, the Trump Administration has located over 157,000 — fulfilling President Trump’s promise to do everything possible to save child victims of human trafficking.

✅ PROMISE KEPT: President Trump immediately resumed construction of the border wall, securing a historic $46.5 billion to build it as part of the Working Families Tax Cuts.
